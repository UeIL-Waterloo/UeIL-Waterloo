Centres & overlap

2.55

Localized valence-bond model: each p orbital can belong to one π bond. An sp² centre has one available p orbital; an sp centre has two perpendicular p orbitals. Selected p gives assignment priority when equivalent choices exist. The selected p phase sets its relative sign in the orbital combination; matching signs give bonding overlap. Inverting one sign gives an out-of-phase combination. Delocalized conjugation is not represented by this localized assignment.

The σ-bond constraint keeps the reference hybrids on the internuclear axis and permits torsion about that axis. Switch it off for free rotation. Actual σ connections are detected from overlapping hybrid lobes, including hybrids other than the reference selection.

Orbital display
0.45

Terminal 1s spheres attach to available hybrid lobes. They appear when hybrid–hybrid overlap is lost and disappear when a σ connection forms. They follow the hybrid display selection and move with their centre. These are schematic orbital surfaces; the overlap indicator is geometric, not a calculated overlap integral or bond energy.

Click or tap a lobe to add an electron; Shift-click or enable removal mode to remove one (maximum two per orbital). Both p lobes share one orbital. Two dots mark a lone pair, which is excluded from bonding. Changing hybridization clears that centre’s electron dots.
